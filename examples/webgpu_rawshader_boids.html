three.jsRaw Shader Boids
Hand-written WGSL through RawShaderNodeMaterial and RawComputeNode, up to one million boids.
The flocking rules of the birds example, on a uniform grid with atomics instead of the all-pairs loop. The pointer is a predator; P releases a bigger bird that hunts through the flock.
Look: drag to turn the camera, wheel to move into the flock, W A S D R F to move. Orbit: drag to turn around the centre, wheel to zoom.
